# Realistic 6-month plan — medium projects first, 6–8 clients/month as the target state

Prepared 24 Oct 2026. Numbers are estimates built from (a) ~250 Upwork profiles read this week, (b) your live job feed, (c) third-party Upwork benchmarks (no official Upwork stats exist for hire rates). Treat ranges as ranges.

## 1. The honest baseline

- **Nobody in the sample reached 6–8 new clients a month in their first quarter.** The Croatian $400K earner has 16 jobs in total. The UK $500K earner took seven years to reach 88. Fast risers in the "Rising Talent" tier reached 5–8 jobs and $20–30K in their first year.
- 6–8 clients/month is reachable **from month 4–6**, and only if the mix is mostly medium fixed-price work ($500–$2,000) plus two or three ongoing hourly retainers. That is exactly the "medium first" shape you proposed, so the goal and the method agree.
- **Fixed-price jobs hire 39% of the time, hourly only 24%** (tracker of 4,583 postings). Medium fixed jobs are also where a zero-review profile is least penalised: the client risks $500, not a six-month retainer.
- Benchmarks for a prepared profile: first contract in **2–6 weeks**; **5–15% of proposals** get a reply; **20–40% of interviews** become hires. So roughly **1 hire per 15–25 well-targeted proposals** at the start, improving to 1 in 8–10 once you have three or four five-star reviews.

## 3. Month-by-month plan

**Month 1 (now – late Nov): get the first 2 reviews.**
- Prerequisites this week: ID verification, Freelancer Plus + 80 Connects (~$22), AWS cert in Certifications, 3 Project Catalog offers (RAG/agent audit $450; MCP server over your database $1,500; paper reproduction $900).
- Send **25–30 proposals** (≈ 450–550 Connects, ~$60–80 total spend over the month). Mix: 60% fixed $500–$2,000; 25% reviews/audits; 15% the best hourly (Egypt, Saudi).
- Accept the first two or three clean small jobs even at $400–$800. The review is the product.
- Realistic outcome: **1–3 contracts, $800–$3,500 booked**, first money lands weeks 4–8 (fixed milestones pay on approval + 5-day hold; hourly pays weekly with ~10-day lag).

**Month 2 (Dec): 2–4 contracts.**
- Ask every finished client for a review and a follow-on hourly phase. The $400K Croatian profile turned a $600 fixed scoping job into a $154K hourly contract with the same client; that is the pattern, not the exception.
- Raise fixed-price bids 20–30% once two 5-star reviews are live.
- Target: **2–4 new contracts, $3,000–$7,000 booked**, one of them an hourly PoC.

**Month 3 (Jan): Rising Talent / Top Rated eligibility.**
- At 90%+ JSS and $1,000+ earned you qualify for Top Rated review (every two weeks). Switch on paid consultations ($120–150 / 30 min) the day the badge lands; they appear in the sample as 3 of every 10 contracts for premium profiles.
- Raise profile rate to $65. Target: **3–5 contracts, $6,000–$10,000 booked.**

**Months 4–6 (Feb–Apr): the 6–8 clients/month shape.**
- Steady state that the research supports: 2–3 ongoing hourly clients (10–20 h/week each at $65–85) + 3–4 fixed jobs/month ($800–$2,500) + 1–2 paid consultations/week.
- Raise rate to $85–95 at Top Rated. Target: **6–8 clients/month, $12,000–$20,000/month booked.**

## 5. When does money actually arrive?

- First contract: **week 2–6** from the day proposals start going out.
- First cash in your account: **week 4–8** (fixed milestone approved → 5-day security period → withdrawal 1–3 days; hourly → weekly billing cycle + 10-day review period).
- Meaningful income (>$3K/month): **month 3** in the realistic case.
- Target state (6–8 clients, $12K+/month): **month 4–6**.

## 6. What decides which column you land in

1. Proposal quality and speed: first line answers the client's question; sent within 24 hours of posting; 5–8 a day on working days.
2. Taking the small jobs in month 1 without arguing about price, then converting them to hourly.
3. Reply time under 2 hours in UK hours. Response time drives invitations, and invitations convert 3–5× better than cold proposals.
4. Not spreading: RAG/agents/evals, self-hosted LLM, Oracle+AI, and research engineering. Decline n8n/no-code and generic chatbot work; it drags the rate down and brings the wrong clients.
5. A short video intro and the AWS badge: both are credibility signals a zero-review profile needs more than an established one does.

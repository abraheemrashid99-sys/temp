## 1. What the research found

Sources: ~250 search cards and ~35 full profiles of Top Rated Plus / Expert-Vetted AI freelancers (highest earners, UK/Europe tier, PhD/research tier, niche specialists, zero-earners for contrast), plus web research on Upwork mechanics. Upwork cut off bulk profile reads partway through with a Terms-of-Service warning on your account, so the sample is ~35 deep reads rather than 100. The patterns below were consistent across all four research tracks.

**What the $75–$200/hr earners do**
1. Title is a positioning statement, not a keyword list. The word **"Production"** appears in most premium titles. Keyword-stuffed titles correlate with the $20–35 tier.
2. First two lines = who you help + what outcome, then one proof line (credentials, numbers). Research/PhD background is a **trust signal in line one, never the headline service**.
3. Overview is 1,700–4,000 characters: hook → proof → what I build → selected results with real numbers and client names → how I work (evals, guardrails, handover) → stack → good-fit filter → CTA.
4. Plain headers or ALL CAPS; the lean, no-emoji style is the one used at $120–$200/hr (Fractional CTO / Architect tier). Emoji walls belong to the volume tier.
5. They sell **paid diagnostics** as the entry rung: $300–$1,500 audits / architecture reviews / 30-min consultations that ladder into $20K–$250K hourly contracts. 5 of 8 premium profiles show this in their work history.
6. 19–20 of 20 skill slots filled, with business-deliverable tags (AI Agent Development, RAG, LLM, Claude, MCP, FastAPI) not academic ones (LaTeX, BERT, Research Papers).
7. Portfolio size does not predict rate ($140/hr with 0 items; $80/hr with 76). Yours (8 items) is already above median.

**How PhD/research people who earn well frame research**
- Publications are quantified, not listed ("100 papers, 7 patents"), once, near the top.
- Research is relabelled as a business capability: "I can distil the maths-dense paper into something your team can ship", "de-risk the decision before you spend six months on it".
- Services they actually sell: architecture of agent/RAG systems, fine-tuning and custom models, paid consultations, "help leadership make sound AI decisions", paper reproduction / research consultation, patent and technical review.
- Academic-only titles ("Lecturer, CS PhD") sit at $40/hr. PhD + commercial noun ("PhD | Industrial & Multimodal Systems") sits at $100–$150.

**What zero-earning profiles share**
- Generic or stuffed title, self-declared "Expert/Senior" with no proof, rate either <$15 or >$60 with no history, academic skill tags, no consultation offer. Your current profile avoids most of this already; the gaps are in positioning, proof placement, awards, and employment detail.

**Rate benchmarks (profiles with real earnings)**
- UK Top Rated AI engineers who actually earn: $55–$80. Europe, $60–85 is the main cluster; $90–120 for Top Rated Plus / Expert-Vetted; $150–200 outliers.
- Rate does not drive earnings. Volume earners at $25; premium earners at $150 with 16 jobs.
